Everything new parents search for, in one friendly place

The first years bring a lot of questions, usually all at once. SillyBaby puts the answers in order, by your child's age and by topic:

  • Feeding: breastfeeding, formula and bottle feeding and starting solids (weaning) at around 6 months.
  • Sleep: how much sleep is normal at each age and the safe sleep basics every carer should know.
  • Development: what most children do by each age in our milestones timeline, and when to ask for a check.
  • Health and safety: what to do about a high temperature, car seats and childproofing your home.
  • Everyday care: nappy (diaper) changing, bath time, teething and potty training.

Gear guides that help you buy less (and buy right)

Babies need less than shops suggest. Our baby gear guide sorts the essentials by age and explains the safety labels to check, from car seats that meet UN R129 (i-Size) in the UK and Europe or FMVSS 213 in the US, to sleep bags that fit properly at the neck and armholes. Where we link to a shop, it is clearly marked, and you always buy directly from the retailer.

Keep a baby diary, and look after you too

Our printable baby diary makes it easy to track feeds, nappies and sleep in the early weeks (handy for midwife, health visitor and paediatrician appointments) and to jot down the firsts you'll want to remember. And because parents matter too, our newborn guide covers the baby blues, postnatal depression and where to get support in the UK and US.

SillyBaby is general information, not medical advice. If you're worried about your baby or child, trust your instincts and contact your midwife, health visitor, GP, paediatrician or NHS 111. In an emergency call 999 (UK), 911 (US) or 112 (EU).

Frequently asked questions

Is SillyBaby written by doctors?
No. SillyBaby is an independent guide site. We summarise official guidance from bodies such as the NHS, CDC, the American Academy of Pediatrics, the WHO and baby-safety charities, and link to it on every page so you can read the original. Always ask your own midwife, health visitor or paediatrician about your child.
Do you cover UK and US advice?
Yes. Where guidance differs between the UK, US and Europe (for example car seat rules or formula advice), we explain both and use both sets of words: diapers and nappies, pacifiers and dummies, strollers and pushchairs.
How does SillyBaby make money?
Some links to shops are affiliate links. If you buy through them, we may earn a commission at no extra cost to you. It never changes our safety advice, and we never invent prices, reviews or test results.
