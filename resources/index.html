How we choose who to list

We only list organisations and people we have checked are real and active, and we link to their official websites. We don't receive anything for these listings. Some creators sell courses or products; that is their business, and you don't need to buy anything to benefit from their free content. If a source disagrees with your own midwife, health visitor or paediatrician, go with the professional who knows your child.

Frequently asked questions

Are parenting influencers reliable?
Some are qualified professionals and many share helpful experience, but social media isn’t personal medical advice. Check health questions against official sources like the NHS, CDC or AAP and your own health professional.
