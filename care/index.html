Your care routine, by age

  • 0–3 m

    Lots of nappy changes, top-and-tail washes, cord care, short tummy time.

  • 3–12 m

    Baths 2–3 times a week, first teeth and brushing, nail trims, sun protection.

  • 1–3 y

    Toothbrushing twice a day, potty readiness, handwashing habits.

  • 3–5 y

    More independence: dressing, toileting and washing with a little help.

Little jobs, quick tips

  • Nails: use baby nail clippers or a nail file when your baby is sleepy or feeding.
  • Sun: keep babies under 6 months out of direct sunlight (NHS); older babies need shade, hats and sunscreen of at least SPF 30 with 4 or 5 star UVA protection.
  • Dummies (pacifiers): if you use one, don't dip it in anything sweet and keep it clean. If breastfeeding, wait until feeding is well established.
  • Hand washing: wash hands after nappy changes and before preparing feeds.

Frequently asked questions

How often should I bath my baby?
The NHS says you don’t need to bath your baby every day; 2 or 3 times a week is fine, with a top-and-tail wash on other days.
